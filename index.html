About

Background

I started out making origami as a kid — folding and selling paper crafts before I'd ever touched a computer. That same hands-on, iterate-until-it-looks-right instinct is how I still work in Blender: a lot of trial and error, testing angles and lighting until a render actually feels right.

These days I do freelance modeling, rendering, and visualization work, along with some Python scripting inside Blender to speed up repetitive parts of the pipeline. Alongside client work, I'm building out a line of 3D-printed STEM education kits.
